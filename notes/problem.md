# The problem

I work from a coworking space a few days a week on a 3-days-a-week monthly plan. Every day I
come in, the receptionist has to check that my plan is still active (usually from a spreadsheet,
sometimes from memory) and then generate a new WiFi code for me in UniFi. When she isn't at her
desk, I just have to wait, and so does everyone else on a day-based plan. This teammate does that
job on WhatsApp: a member sends a message, it checks their plan and its rules, and sends back a
WiFi code that works on up to 2 devices. It also tells members when their plan ends and answers
questions about opening hours and plans. Anything involving money, renewals or day passes still
goes to reception. I deliberately left these out of the solution to keep it stupid simple
